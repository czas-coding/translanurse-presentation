Why TranslaNurse Refuses to Suggest Medicines

Taking the wrong drug, improper dosage, or self-medicating can mask serious illnesses, induce drug-drug interactions, or trigger organ injury. TranslaNurse provides strictly non-pharmacological comfort measures, symptom explanations, and red-flag limits while routing patients to licensed physicians.

✓ No Prescriptions ✓ No Physical Diagnosis ✓ Direct Physician Routing
🔒 100% CLIENT-SIDE PRIVACY LOCALSTORAGE ARCHITECTURE

Zero External Servers or Commercial Tracking

Lab results and text queries stay locally on the user's device via browser memory (localStorage). TranslaNurse operates with zero user profiling, zero tracking cookies, and zero commercial databases—plus 1-click Print Editorial Patient Guide & .TXT Export.
