PROTOCOL 01 // PHARMACOLOGICAL SAFETY

Zero-Medication Guardrail

Why does TranslaNurse refuse to suggest medicines? Improper dosage or self-medicating can mask serious illness, trigger drug interactions, or cause organ injury. We provide strictly non-pharmacological comfort measures and route prescriptions to physicians.

Where are lab results and queries stored? Strictly on the patient's own device via browser memory (localStorage). TranslaNurse operates with zero tracking cookies, zero user profiling, and zero external commercial databases.
